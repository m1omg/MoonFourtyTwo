# Bug fixes — October 2026

All the bugs you reported are fixed, plus several more found along the way. This report doesn't
spoil anything past the first pub and the first dark scene.

- **Credits:** none spent on any of these fixes. Higgsfield and Krea balances are unchanged.
- **Live:** everything below is merged into `ccr-2a407a47-pkyyrs` and online.
- **Parts:**
  - The first part (up to 5 October) was made on `claude/funny-rubin-44ks5l`.
  - The second part (6–9 October) is the second half of this report.
  - Where things stand now: `docs/HANDOFF.md`.

## Other bugs found and fixed

**First pub**

- Ežo said "drink up" _after_ you had already finished your beer.
- Rounds Ežo ordered were described as waiting "on the counter" but appeared straight in your
  pockets. They now actually wait on the counter until you take them.
- Toasting with only one glass left gave you a free extra drink.
- Ežo could point at something on the wall that wasn't visible yet.
- Looking at the clock gave an answer that had nothing to do with the clock.

**Later chapters**

- In the next chapter, glasses you picked up stayed on the tables, and a "sit with a glass" prompt
  didn't actually put your beer down.
- In a later chapter, a pursuer could walk through walls, a counter and furniture. I wrote a check
  that tests every chapter for this. Across the whole game, creatures now pass only through doors
  that open for them.
- A sound in a late chapter played from the wrong place.

**Second pass: every later chapter read through**

- In one chapter, there are exactly as many of a certain item as its puzzle needs. Using one at
  the wrong moment wasted it and made the chapter impossible to finish. It now comes back to you.
- In another chapter, the one hint telling you what to do next was said only twice. If you missed
  it you could wait forever; it now repeats.
- In two chapters, progress made right before a blackout (a solved puzzle, collected items) was
  lost. It is now saved the moment you make it.
- The rest of the later chapters checked out: no other softlocks, no unsafe respawn points, and
  no other way for creatures to pass through walls.

## How it was checked

- Lint, type check, and 78 unit tests.
- The full browser test suite, which covers every chapter's main path: 37 passed, 1 skipped by
  design. The pub, frozen-pub and save tests were then re-run on the final build: 3 passed.
- Screenshots of the characters, the glasses and the dark scenes, taken before and after the
  fixes.

## 6–9 October: audit, new features and speed

Everything here is live. Saves from before stay compatible.

### New

- **Save slots.** „Uložiť hru" in the pause menu keeps your last checkpoint in one of three
  slots. „Načítať hru" (pause menu and title screen) lists the slots and the five most recent
  checkpoints, which are kept automatically. „Pokračovať" works as before.
- **Drinks and food:** each has its own first-person movement. A shot is knocked back, a bottle
  is tipped up, and bread or pastry goes in two bites.
- **Phones held upright** work too. The stick and buttons sit above the hotbar, and the
  subtitles move to the top.
- **Optional touch scheme:** Nastavenia → Dotykové ovládanie → „Ťahaním chodíš, joystick otáča
  kameru". The default is unchanged.
- **The pub toilet is furnished:**
  - a bowl with a high cistern and pull chain, and toilet paper;
  - a urinal and a washbasin with taps and soap;
  - a bin, a floor drain and a „WC" plate by the door.

  All of it is built in code, so no credits were spent.

- **Being caught no longer looks like drinking too much.** You see what got you, and the screen
  says „Niečo ťa dostalo."

### Chapters 1 and 2

**Ežo:**

- His mug stands on the table between sips.
- His hands no longer cross or sink into the mug. The same pose is used for everyone sitting at
  a table.

**Vierka's keys:**

- Walk up to the bar in front of her, or use „Vypýtať si od Vierky kľúče". You ask, and she
  hands them over.
- They are saved at once, so a blackout later doesn't take them.
- **Right after the two caught you:** she gives them to you at once, Ežo stops talking, and
  nothing moves meanwhile.
- **When you wake up with the keys:** the game tells you so, and the front door offers
  „Odomknúť kľúčmi".

**The two card players:**

- They come at a third of their old speed, and a little slower after each catch.
- They no longer move while you can see them at the sides of the screen.
- They don't walk through each other or leave invisible blocks where they first sat.
- They can't reach you through the closed WC door.
- They move smoothly on any screen.
- After a blackout you wake by Ežo's table. Nothing moves until you step away, and Ežo repeats
  his advice.

**The rooms:**

- **The front door couldn't be reached from inside.** A menu board, the corner table and the
  card players closed off the way. It is open again, and a new test walks the player's own body
  to the door.
- **The toilet:** stepping in and straight back out no longer leaves its door shut with nothing
  happening when you come back.
- **Doors:**
  - The cubicle's lamp hung exactly in the plane of its door, which left that door unlit. The
    lamp hangs mid-room now.
  - The WC and street doors were a black slab from the pub. They are worn veneer now, with a
    small lamp by the WC door.
- **„Obmedziť záblesky":** the frozen pub's lamps dim instead of going black.

**Smaller fixes:**

- Pause → „Do hlavného menu" no longer freezes the next game.
- The soap sits in a dish and the till stands on the counter.
- The jukebox and the red neon stay off in the frozen pub.
- Vierka keeps her pose after loading.

### Everywhere

- **A full hotbar** no longer makes things you pick up vanish. A seventh kind gets a seventh slot
  (keys 7–9), which closes again when used up.
- **Progress is saved the moment you make it**, even near danger. A drink you were in the middle
  of counts as not drunk.
- **After a new version goes online,** moving to the next part no longer hangs on
  „Načítavam…". The game saves, reloads and carries on. A graphics reset on a phone does the same.
- **Menus:**
  - No pause over a question, a note or the ending.
  - The pause no longer reopens right after „Pokračovať".
  - Answers take the number keys on Slovak keyboards.
  - „Uložené" shows in the pause menu.
  - „Nová hra" asks first when there is a saved game.
  - The ending credits can be clicked.
  - Right-click no longer opens the browser menu.
- **Keys:** prompts and hints name the keys you bound.
- **Sitting down** while still holding a movement key no longer pulls you straight back up.
- **Aiming** at a small thing next to a big one picks the small one.
- **Voice lines:** one that is slow to download no longer holds the story up. The subtitle
  shows without the voice.
- **Falling out of the world** puts you back at the last checkpoint.
- **Mouse buttons:** any control can be bound to the middle or side mouse buttons (Myš 3, 4, 5)
  in Nastavenia → Ovládanie. The side buttons no longer take the browser back, out of the game.
- **Running:** only the lighter goes out, with a sound and a hint.

### Later chapters

Every later chapter was read through and played by the tests. Without saying what they hold, the
fixes in chapters 3–12 cover:

- something that could get stuck for good;
- lines that were cut off or never said, and hints that never played;
- progress a blackout took back, and one chapter a blackout could leave unfinishable;
- places to fall through or off, and light shining through walls;
- objects floating above tables or sunk into them;
- sounds that never stopped.

### Speed, with the same picture

- **Shadows** are drawn once per frame. On high they used to be drawn three or four times.
- **Characters out of view** are no longer drawn.
- **The frozen pub's TV** only renders while you can see it.
- **Result:** seated in the pub on high, a frame now draws about 16 % fewer triangles.

- 38 browser tests. They include new ones for:
  - the walk to the front door and the other walkways, using the player's own body;
  - getting the keys right after a catch;
  - peeking into the toilet.
- Each part went online only after CI passed. The last run, for the keys fix, passed everything.
